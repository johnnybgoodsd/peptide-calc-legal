PEPTIDE INJECTION TRACKER

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 15, 2026

The short version: Peptide Injection Tracker does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal data. Everything you enter stays on your device.

Data we collect

None. The app does not collect, transmit, or store any personal data on external servers. We have no analytics, no advertising, no third-party tracking SDKs, and no account system.

Where your information lives

Any data you create in the app — peptide names, injection logs, schedules, vial records, sites, notes, and settings — is stored only on your device. It never leaves your phone unless you choose to export or share it yourself using your device's own sharing features.

Face ID / biometrics

If you enable the optional app lock, Face ID or Touch ID is handled entirely by Apple on your device. We never see, receive, or store your biometric data.

No accounts

The app does not require you to create an account or provide your name, email, or any other identifying information to use it.

Third parties

We do not share data with third parties because we do not collect any. The app does not integrate advertising networks or analytics providers.

Children

This app is rated 18+ and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect any information from anyone.

Medical disclaimer

Peptide Injection Tracker is a recordkeeping and educational-reference tool for research and educational purposes only. It does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment, and does not recommend doses. Always consult a qualified clinician before making medical decisions.

Changes to this policy

If our practices ever change, we will update this page and revise the date above.
