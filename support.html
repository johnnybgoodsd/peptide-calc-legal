Frequently asked questions

What does the app do?

Peptide Injection Tracker lets you keep a private injection log, review a calendar and site history, and use optional reference tools. Pro adds multiple peptides, flexible schedules, active-vial inventory, and PDF/CSV exports.

Is this medical advice?

No. Peptide Injection Tracker is a recordkeeping and educational-reference tool. It is not a medical device, does not calculate an amount to administer, and does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, treatment, or dosage guidance. Always consult a qualified clinician before making medical decisions.

Does Pro include a free trial?

No. You can keep using the free version, or choose a monthly or annual Pro subscription. The full price and billing period are shown before purchase.

Where is my data stored?

Everything you enter stays on your device. We don't run servers, accounts, ads, or analytics, and we never collect or see your data. See our Privacy Policy.

How do I manage or cancel my subscription?

Pro is an auto-renewable subscription billed through your Apple Account. Manage or cancel it anytime in Settings → your name → Subscriptions on your iPhone, or from Settings → Manage Subscription inside the app. Cancel at least 24 hours before a renewal to avoid being charged for the next period.

How do I delete my data?

Use Settings → Erase All Data in the app, or delete the app from your iPhone — either removes all on-device data.
