Frequently asked questions

What does the app do?

You enter your vial amount, mixing volume, and target dose, and the app shows exactly how many units to draw, the concentration, and how many doses are in the vial — with the full formula shown so you can verify the math yourself. It also lets you log your injections, rotate injection sites on a body map, and track vials and discard dates.

Is this medical advice?

No. Peptide Calculator + Tracker is a calculation and tracking tool for educational and informational purposes only. It is not a medical device and does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment, and it does not recommend doses. Always verify every number against your own reference and consult a qualified clinician before making any decision. Use at your own risk.

Where is my data stored?

Everything you enter stays on your device. We don't run servers, accounts, ads, or analytics, and we never collect or see your data. See our Privacy Policy.

How do I manage or cancel my subscription?

Pro is an auto-renewable subscription billed through your Apple Account. Manage or cancel it anytime in Settings → your name → Subscriptions on your iPhone, or from Settings → Manage Subscription inside the app. Cancel at least 24 hours before a renewal to avoid being charged for the next period.

How do I delete my data?

Use Settings → Erase All Data in the app, or delete the app from your iPhone — either removes all on-device data.
