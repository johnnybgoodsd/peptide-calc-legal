Frequently asked questions

What does the app do?

You enter your vial amount, mixing volume, and target dose, and the app shows exactly how many units to draw, the concentration, and how many doses are in the vial, with the full formula shown so you can verify the math yourself. It also lets you save protocols and log injections.

Is this medical advice?

No. Peptide Calc & Dose Tracker is a calculation and tracking tool for research and educational purposes only. It does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment, and does not recommend doses. Always consult a qualified clinician before making medical decisions.

Is my data private?

Yes. Everything stays on your device. The app collects no data, has no accounts, and uses no analytics or tracking. See our Privacy Policy.

How do I unlock the app if I forget?

The optional lock uses your device's Face ID or Touch ID. If biometrics fail, use your device passcode as configured in iOS.
